Our Materials and Substrate Guide

Laser manufacturing depends heavily on thermal absorption, substrate density, and surface reflection. We calibrate our 55W CO₂ and 10W diode systems to deliver clean edge profiles with zero scorching.

1. Wood / Organic Substrates

  • Baltic Birch / Basswood Plywood: Premium multi-ply sheet goods with tight grain and low glue content. Ideal for interlocking architectural models, geometric art, and structural joinery.
  • Hardwoods (Oak, Walnut, Cherry): Yields rich, dark contrast when raster engraved and razor-sharp eges when cut. Perfect for custom signage, plaques, and luxury wooden goods.
  • Cardboard / Chipboard / Vellum: Low-density paper substrates processed at high speed for rapid architectural mockups and packaging prototypes.

2. Synthetic and Specialty Materials

  • Cast Acrylic (Clear, Opaque, and Color): The gold standard for laser cutting. Flame-polishes to a glossy crystal-clear edge on vector cuts and etches to a bright frosted white contrast.
  • Genuine Leather / Suede: Vaporizes cleanly along paths to produce custom branded patches, apparel tags, and journal covers with a classic dark burnished edge.
  • Anodized Aluminum / Coated Metals: Etched directly or marked using thermal bonding agents to produce durable serial tags, equipment badges, and high-contrast metal plates.

3. Technical Laser Parameters

  • Vector Cutting vs. Raster Engraving: Vector toolpaths cut completely through media along single-pixel paths, while raster engraving sweeps back and forth like an inkjet printer to burn pixelated images.
  • Focal Length and Air Assist Calibration: High-pressure air assist blows away smoke and debris during cuts, preventing charred edges and maintaining optical lens clarity.
  • Kerf Allowance and Interlocking Tolerances: We calculate the precise laser beam width (kerf) in CAD to ensure press-fit box joints and interlocking assemblies fit snugly without glue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions before starting your laser cutting project.

What file formats do you accept for laser cutting?

We prefer vector files such as SVG, DXF, AI, or PDF. For raster engraving projects, high-resolution PNG or JPG images work best.

Do I need to convert text to paths or outlines?

Yes! Please convert all text to paths, curves, or outlines in your vector software before submitting your file to prevent font substitution errors.

What is the maximum cutting thickness for your laser systems?

Our 55W CO₂ system can cleanly cut up to 20mm acrylic and thick hardwoods in a single pass, while our diode system handles lighter materials like thin plywood and cardstock.

Can you engrave photos or custom artwork?

Absolutely! We can process high-contrast raster images and grayscale graphics to burn realistic portraits, logos, and artwork onto wood and leather.

Can you assist with vector file preparation or design?

Yes, we offer custom vector design and layout optimization in case your sketches or ideas need to be converted into a clean, laser-ready cutting file.
