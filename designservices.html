Our Design Service Offerings

Whether you need a full ground-up engineering design or quick adjustments to existing files, we offer tailored services across every stage of the design cycle.

1. 3D Parametric CAD and Mechanical Modeling

  • Parametric Design: Building 3D models driven by precise dimensions and constraints, allowing easy adjustments to hole sizes, wall thicknesses, or component fits without starting over.
  • Custom Enclosures / Functional Parts: Designing tailored snap-fit housings, mounting brackets, adapters, and multi-part mechanical assemblies engineered specifically for additive manufacturing.
  • Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM): Optimizing part geometry with self-supporting overhang angles, proper print bed contact areas, integrated chamfers, and stress-relieved radii to minimize print supports and maximize strength.

2. Reverse Engineering and Part Replication

  • Broken Part Replacement: Re-creating obsolete or broken mechanical parts (gears, latches, custom brackets, worn plastic tabs) using digital callipers and precision measurement.
  • Physical to Digital Translation: Measuring existing physical objects and reproducing clean CAD models ready for 3D printing or laser cutting modifications.

3. 2D Vector Optimization / Image Conversion

  • Raster to Vector Conversion (Vectorization): Converting hand-drawn sketches, raster JPEGs, or logos into clean, infinitely scalable vector paths required for laser cutting, pen plotting, and sticker contour cutting.
  • Kerf Allowance / Cut Path Preparation: Offsetting vector paths to compensate for laser beam width (kerf) or vinyl blade drag, ensuring tight press-fit joints, clean interlocking slots, and zero gap errors.
  • Custom Contour Bleed Setup: Adding white borders, cut lines, and registration marks around full-color artwork for accurate sticker kiss-cuts and die-cuts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our design and CAD services.

What if I only have a rough sketch or an idea?

That is plenty to get started! We can take hand drawings with key measurements, rough photos, or conceptual ideas and turn them into fully functional digital 3D models or vector files.

Can you duplicate a broken or worn-out plastic part?

Yes! Bring in or send us the original broken part. We use precision digital calipers to measure key dimensions, reconstruct the original geometry in Autodesk Fusion 360, and 3D print a replacement.

Do I get to keep the original CAD or vector source files?

Absolutely. Once the design phase is completed, we can provide you with standard STEP, STL, DXF, or SVG files so you own your design assets.

What file formats do I need to send if I already have a design?

For 3D printing, we accept STEP, 3MF, or STL files. For laser cutting, pen plotting, or sticker cutting, vector files like SVG, DXF, PDF, or AI are ideal.

How do you ensure 3D printed parts will fit together properly?

We design with precise mechanical tolerances built in-typically leaving 0.15mm to 0.30mm clearance for moving parts and snap-fits depending on the material and machine used.
