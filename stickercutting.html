Our Materials and Vinyl Guide

Sticker manufacturing relies on proper adhesive tack, face-film flexibility, and laminate protection. Selecting the correct vinyl and finish combination ensures your stickers peel easily and survive real-world conditions.

1. Vinyl and Film Classifications

  • Printable White Gloss Vinyl: The industry standard for vibrant, full-color graphic stickers. Features high-opacity white backing and a glossy finish that makes colors pop.
  • Printable White Matte Vinyl: Provides a smooth, non-reflective finish with an upscale aesthetic. Excellent for minimalist branding, product packaging labels, and aesthetic notebook stickers.
  • Holographic / Specialty Sparkle Vinyl: Reflective metallic films embedded with rainbow or glitter patterns that shift color under light, adding an eye-catching backdrop to custom designs.
  • Permanent Adhesive Outdoor Vinyl: Heavy-duty colored and patterned vinyl engineered with industrial-grade permanent adhesive for long-term outdoor exposure.

2. Protective Laminates and Finishes

  • Gloss UV-Protective Overlaminate: A clear protective layer applied over printed vinyl to shield ink from UV fading, water, scratches, and chemical cleaners.
  • Matte Protective Overlaminate: Provides a soft-touch, anti-glare finish while adding heavy-duty scratch and weather protection to outdoor decals.
  • Unlaminated Standard Finish: Cost-effective option optimized for indoor use, notebooks, packaging seals, and short-term promotional handouts.

3. Cutting and Technical Parameters

  • Kiss-Cutting vs. Die-Cutting: Kiss-cuts slice only through the vinyl layer while leaving the paper backing intact (ideal for sticker sheets), whereas die-cuts slice completely through both layers for individual custom-shaped stickers.
  • Blade Depth / Pressure Calibration: We fine-tune blade pressure in software based on media thickness to prevent tearing the liner or leaving un-cut corner fibers.
  • Bleed / Safe Margins: We program a 2mm color bleed around vector cut lines to ensure zero white borders show if slight mechanical shifting occurs during cutting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our sticker cutting service.

What is the difference between kiss-cut and die-cut stickers?

Kiss-cuts slice through the vinyl but leave the backing paper whole, making them great for sticker sheets with multiple designs. Die-cuts cut completely through both the vinyl and backing paper to give you individual stickers shaped to your artwork.

What file formats do you accept for sticker cutting?

We prefer vector files like SVG, AI, PDF, or high-resolution PNG files with transparent backgrounds (at least 300 DPI) accompanied by a vector cut contour line.

Are your stickers waterproof and outdoor-safe?

Yes! When printed on our premium vinyl and finished with our UV-protective overlaminate, our stickers are fully waterproof, dishwasher safe, and rated for outdoor weather resistance.

Can you help design my sticker or add a border?

Absolutely! We can add custom offset white borders, drop shadows, or adjust your artwork layout using our design services before cutting.

What is your minimum order quantity for custom stickers?

We accommodate everything from small single-batch custom runs for testing up to large bulk production orders for businesses and events.
